Technology Overview

What is DreamWare?

DreamWare is a proprietary operating environment developed internally at SleeepScope. It does not run on conventional hardware in a conventional sense. Rather, it interfaces directly with the neurological activity of a sedated patient and renders their subconscious as a navigable file system.

To the specialist, it looks like an old computer. Folders. Files. A cursor. A blinking prompt. Simple, by design — the complexity is entirely on the other side of the screen.

How it works — simplified

Memory as Data

The DreamWare interface translates a patient's subconscious memories into readable files and navigable directories. A childhood home becomes a folder. A suppressed trauma becomes a locked archive. A recurring nightmare becomes a corrupted file the system cannot open — yet.

Your job as a SleeepScope specialist is to explore, read, and where necessary, modify what you find. Treatment is not always gentle. Some doors are locked for a reason.
